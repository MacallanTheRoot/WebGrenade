# WebGrenade Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** March 22, 2026

## 1. Introduction

WebGrenade ("we," "us," or "the extension") is developed by MacallanTheRoot. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the information we collect, access, or use when you install and use the WebGrenade extension on Firefox or Chrome.

WebGrenade is built on a privacy-by-design model. We believe that your browsing data should remain your own. Our business model does not involve selling, renting, or monetizing your data.

## 2. Data We Access and How We Use It

WebGrenade requires specific browser permissions to function. We only request permissions that are essential for the tools we provide. Here is a detailed breakdown of what we access:

| Permission | Tool or Module Using It | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **activeTab and scripting** | Color Studio, Volume Booster, Dark Mode, Popup Blocker | To interact with and modify the content of the currently active tab upon your direct request (for example, to pick a color, boost audio, or inject dark mode styling). This data is processed locally. |
| **storage** | All Modules | To save your preferences, tool configurations, color palettes, RSS feed subscriptions, and customized settings locally on your device. |
| **cookies** | Cookie Manager | To enable you to view, add, edit, and delete cookies for the current domain for web development and debugging purposes. |
| **history** | History Cleaner | To allow you to search for and delete specific browsing history entries by domain or date range upon your direct request. We do not read your history for any other purpose. |
| **declarativeNetRequest** | User-Agent Switcher, Popup Blocker | To dynamically modify HTTP request headers (for example, User-Agent) or block specific network requests (for example, malicious popups) entirely locally. |
| **tabs** | RSS Reader, Media Center, Image Search | To open new tabs for downloaded media links, full-text RSS articles, or reverse image search results. |
| **contextMenus** | Fake Input Filler, Image Search | To add quick-access shortcuts to your browser's right-click menu. |

## 3. Data Storage

All data processed or generated by your use of WebGrenade, including tool settings, saved palettes, and RSS subscriptions, is stored locally on your device using the browser local storage API.

This also includes Quick Notes content, the latest captured markdown clip payload, focus timer state, and other module-specific preferences that the extension needs to restore your workflow after reopening the popup.

**We do not run any backend servers and we do not transmit any of your personal or browsing data to ourselves or any outside party.**

## 4. Third-Party Services and APIs

WebGrenade acts as an interface to several public and third-party APIs. When you actively use these specific tools, your data is sent directly from your browser to the respective service:

- **is.gd and TinyURL:** When you request a URL to be shortened.
- **QR Server API:** When you generate a QR code from a URL.
- **External Lyrics API:** When you search for song lyrics.
- **RapidAPI:** Used for advanced media extraction if you have configured your own API key in the extension settings.

These requests are made directly from your browser. We do not proxy or log these requests. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of these respective third-party providers.
